# Coordinated Handoff

When a durable coordinator invokes this skill, return a version-2 document
conforming to the public [Agentic Delta schema](https://github.com/samba/agentic-delta/blob/main/skills/kanban/references/specialist-handoff.schema.json).

Preserve the assigned `specialist_class`, class version, engagement role,
review purpose and plan item, worker, gate, applicability, recommendation, and
execution status. Use only canonical values; finding severity belongs on each
finding, not in a compound gate result.

For assurance, turn every material contribution into an obligation tied to a
frozen tenet, with lifecycle stage, verification method, owner, and artifact.
Control reviewers independently verify obligations and evidence rather than
creating new assurance work. Evidence identifies the criterion, artifact and
exact revision, probe, result, producer, environment, and location.

Early enrollment may propose sourced project principles or verifiable tenets.
Existing-codebase review returns goal-relative risks, deficiencies, evidence,
and earliest repair stage. Bug triage returns applicability, impact, urgency,
risk, and rationale. An evidenced `not-applicable` disposition is valid.

The coordinator alone persists authoritative state and owns transitions,
permissions, priority, risk acceptance, and delivery. Only its committed
ingestion receipt establishes acceptance.
